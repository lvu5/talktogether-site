About TalkTogether

Built for real conversations.

TalkTogether started with a simple idea: meeting new people should feel easy, human, and safe — whether you want to practice a language, find people nearby, or just talk.

People first

No endless feeds. TalkTogether is designed around live, one-to-one and group conversations with real people.

Privacy by default

We only show approximate distance, never your exact location, and you can delete your account anytime from the app.

Safe spaces

Blocking, reporting, and room moderation tools help keep conversations respectful for everyone.

Our story

TalkTogether is an independent project made by a small team who believe the internet can still be a place to make genuine connections. We wanted an app where you could jump into a conversation in your own language, meet someone across the world, or join a community room — without the noise.

What you can do

  • Match with people by language or approximate distance
  • Join and host community rooms
  • Chat with text, images, and GIFs
  • Talk with voice, video, and screen sharing where available
  • Sign in with Apple, Google, or as a guest
